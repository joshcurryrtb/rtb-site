Two nights. Two lifts.

Perfect the lift most people get wrong.

The deadlift and the squat build more strength than anything else in the gym, and they're the two lifts most people do badly or avoid. Pick your night and a coach fixes yours.

Tuesday night

Perfect your deadlift

Setup, brace, pull. The coach builds your deadlift from the floor up and sets the weight so your legs and glutes do the work, not your lower back. Lift heavy without the fear.

Thursday night

Perfect your squat

Stance, depth, bar position. The coach dials the squat in for your hips and knees and stays on every set until it clicks. No more guessing, no more sore knees from bad form.

We keep a few spots in each of these classes for people trying RTB for the first time. When they're taken for the week, the calendar shows the next one.

Questions

Before you book

Is it really free?

Yes. The free pass is one real class, no card, nothing to pay on the night. One pass per person. We'd rather you feel the difference than take our word for it.

Why only Tuesday and Thursday nights?

Those are the deadlift and squat nights, the two lifts most people want to learn properly. We hold a few spots in each for first timers so the class stays capped at 12.

Will I be thrown in with people who've trained for years?

You'll be in the same room, not on the same weights. The coach sets your loads and stays on you. Members were all new once and they know it.

What do I bring?

Runners, a drink bottle, clothes you can squat in. We supply the rest.

Can I get a second free pass?

No. One is enough to know. After that it's the 21 Day Strength Reset, which is unlimited classes for three weeks.

I've got a bad back or a dodgy knee.

Tell the coach at the door and they'll work around it. If you're currently under treatment, check with your physio first.
