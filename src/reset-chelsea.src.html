Questions

Before you book

Is the call a sales call?

No. It's fifteen minutes about you. Where you're at, what you want, and whether the Reset fits. If it does, we tell you how to start. If it doesn't, we tell you that too.

How does the guarantee work?

Your coach records your working weights on the main lifts from your first sessions. Come three times a week (nine sessions across the 21 days) and follow your program. On day 21 we compare. If your numbers haven't gone up and you join as a member, your first week is free.

I've never lifted weights. Is that a problem?

It's the most common starting point we see. The Reset eases you in with a coach on every set and a program written for exactly where you are. Nobody is thrown in the deep end.

Why only 10 spots a month?

Classes are capped at 12, and every new person gets real coaching time in their first three weeks. Ten a month is what we can coach properly. When they're gone, the next intake is the following month.

Will I get bulky?

No. Bulk comes from eating far more than you burn for a long time. Lifting three or four times a week with normal food makes you stronger and more capable. That's it.

What happens after 21 days?

You retest and decide. Most people join. If you don't, you keep your program and your numbers. No lock in means exactly that.
