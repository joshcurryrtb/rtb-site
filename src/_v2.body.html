A note from Josh

Don't trust us. Test us.

If you're reading this, you've probably tried a few things already. Bootcamps. Apps. A membership you stopped using by March. You worked hard, and nobody could tell you why your body barely changed.

I'm an exercise scientist. I built RTB because most gyms sell effort, not direction. Random workouts and endless cardio feel productive. They rarely change how you look or how strong you are.

At RTB every session is coached, every group is capped at 12, and every program is written for the person doing it. We track your lifts and we scan your body, so you can see it working instead of hoping it is.

So don't take my word for it. Book the call, come in, and judge us on 21 days. Bring your scepticism. Watch how we coach. If we haven't earned your trust by the end, you still walk away stronger, with a program that's yours to keep.
