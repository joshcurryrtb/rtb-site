The bit everyone gets wrong

It tells you the weight. And when to go heavier.

Most people guess. Too light and nothing changes. Too heavy and form goes, then the back goes. Either way you stall and blame yourself.

The app removes the guess. Every lift has a working max, an estimate of your 1 rep max built from what you have actually lifted. Every set is written as a percentage of it, and the app turns that into kilos before you touch the bar. 60% of a 190 working max is 114. Load it. Go.

Hit the reps at the weight and your working max moves up. Miss them and it holds. You never decide. The numbers decide.

Testing week

Retest. Reset. Go again.

Every block ends with a testing week. You retest the main lifts, the working max updates, and the next block is built off what you can lift today, not what you did in March.

Your rep maxes, PRs and estimated 1RM live in the app for good. Set a goal number and watch the gap close.

Every exercise, shown

Never guess a movement.

Tap any exercise and there is a video and the points of performance. Chest supported row, DB RDL, reverse lunge. You see it, you read it, you do it.

Log every set as you go. The app tracks volume, minutes, streaks and readiness so you can see the work stacking up week on week.
