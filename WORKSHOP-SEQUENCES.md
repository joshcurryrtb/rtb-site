# Workshop Follow-Up Sequences — Hapana CRM

Workshop: **Friday 11 September, 6:30–8:15 PM, RTB Chelsea Heights**

The whole point of these is show rate. Free workshops run ~50% show without reminders and
~75–80% with a proper sequence. Every message below is short on purpose — long reminder
messages get ignored.

---

### 3. T-3 days (Tue 8 Sept, ~10am) — Email
**Subject:** The three things that wreck most people's deadlift

> Hi [First],
>
> Three days until Friday. Here's what we're actually fixing:
>
> **The hips don't move first.** Most people bend their back before their hips go anywhere.
> That's the whole ballgame — get this and your back stops complaining.
>
> **No brace.** Almost nobody has been taught how to breathe and brace under load. It takes
> about ten minutes to learn and it's the thing that protects your spine.
>
> **Copying someone else's squat.** Your hips and ankles aren't theirs. Your stance shouldn't
> be either.
>
> We'll sort all three on Friday. 6:30pm, 21/107 Wells Rd.

> Josh

### 4. T-1 day (Thu 10 Sept, ~5pm) — SMS  ⬅ **the one that matters most**

spent 90 minutes with you.*

## Setup notes for Hapana

- Build these as an **automation/journey** attached to the workshop event, not manual sends.
- The T-1 day SMS and the day-of 4:30 PM SMS are the two that carry the show rate. If Hapana
  limits how many automated SMS you can send, keep those two above all others.
- You need **three segments**: booked → attended, booked → no-show, attended → bought.
  Mark attendance on the night or the post-workshop messages go to the wrong people.
- SMS must come from a number Josh can actually receive replies on. Half the value of the
  T-1 message is the "reply Y".
- Australian SMS: include an opt-out on the first message of the sequence if Hapana doesn't
  append one automatically.
