Why These Two Lifts

If You Only Ever Did Two Exercises,
These Are The Two

A squat and a deadlift, done properly, train nearly every muscle you own in a handful of sets. They build the strength that keeps you lifting kids, carrying shopping and getting off the floor at 70. Nothing else in a gym comes close for the time it takes.

Which is exactly why doing them badly, or avoiding them entirely, costs you more than any other mistake in training.

01

Most Muscle, Least Time

Two lifts covering legs, glutes, back, core and grip. If you're time poor, this is the highest return on every minute you spend training.

02

Real World Strength

Standing up and picking things up are the two movements you do every day of your life. Train them properly and everything outside the gym gets easier.

03

A Back That Holds Up

A correctly loaded deadlift is one of the best things you can do for a back that niggles. Done wrong, it's one of the worst. The difference is technique.

Sound Familiar?

Why Most People
Never Touch The Bar

01

"I've got a bad back." Usually the reason to learn these lifts, not avoid them. What you actually need is someone watching your hips, not a lifetime of leg press.

02

"I don't know if I'm doing it right." Almost nobody does. You can't see your own back angle, and the mirror lies. Ninety minutes of eyes on you fixes what YouTube can't.

03

"Someone showed me once, years ago." And you've been repeating whatever you half-remembered ever since, usually with a rounding lower back you have no idea is happening.

04

"I'd feel stupid trying it in a busy gym." Fair. So we're doing it in a closed room with 11 other people in exactly the same boat and a coach whose entire job is to make it click.

What You'll Walk Out With

Four Things You'll Have
By The End Of The Night

01

Your Squat Setup

Stance width, foot angle, depth and bar position dialled in for your hips and ankles, not a generic cue off the internet. Everybody's squat looks slightly different, and yours should too.

02

The Hinge

The single skill that separates a deadlift that builds you from one that hurts you: loading your hips instead of your lower back. Most people have never been taught it.

03

How To Brace

The breathing and bracing pattern that turns your midsection into a solid trunk under load. This is the part that protects your spine, and it takes about ten minutes to learn.

04

Numbers To Start With

You won't leave with theory. You'll leave knowing roughly what you can squat and deadlift today, and where to start on Monday.

Questions

Before You Book

Is it really free?

Yes. No card, no deposit, nothing to pay on the night. We're running these to get to know people in the area and to film some coaching footage. You get the coaching, we get to show what we do.

Will I get a hard sell at the end?

No. I'll take five minutes at the end to explain how training here works and what our 21 Day Strength Reset is, because some people will want to know. If that's not you, say no thanks and enjoy the session. It costs you nothing either way.

I've never lifted a barbell in my life. Am I going to look like an idiot?

Everyone in the room is there for the same reason. We start with an empty bar and a lot of people don't add weight to it at all. Nobody's watching you except the coach, and that's the point.

I've got a dodgy back / knee / shoulder. Can I still come?

If you're managing something and you're cleared to train, yes. Tell us when you arrive and we'll work around it. If you're currently injured and under treatment, check with your physio first.

Can I bring a friend?

Please do, just book them their own spot so we don't go over twelve.

What if I can't make it on the night?

Let us know as early as you can so someone else can take the spot. We'll tell you when the next one is on.

Do I need to be fit already?

No. This is a technique session, not a fitness test. If you can walk up a flight of stairs you'll be fine.

The date I want isn't there. Are you running more?

Yes. We'll keep adding dates as these fill up. Everything currently open is in the calendar above, so if nothing suits, check back in a week or drop us a line at chelseaheights@rtbgym.com.au and we'll let you know when the next batch goes up.
