How it works

Most people don't have an effort problem.
They have a direction problem.

You are showing up. You are trying. Without a program, a coach and real numbers, progress stalls and then motivation goes with it. RTB fixes the direction.

01

Full body. Every session.

No chest day, back day, leg day. Every session is built around one main lift and hits everything. Miss a day and nothing waits a week.

02

The program tells you the weight.

Every set is written off your working max and shown in kilos before you touch the bar. You never guess a weight and you never guess when to go heavier.

03

Test. Reset. Go again.

New program every month, alternating strength and hypertrophy. Every block ends with a testing week so your numbers stay honest.

Why no cardio

Three hours a week.
Spend them on the thing that works.

If you have three or four hours a week to train, an hour on a treadmill is an hour you did not spend getting stronger. Strength training builds muscle, muscle changes your shape and lifts your metabolism, and it is the only thing in the gym that keeps paying you back at 40, 50 and 70. We took the cardio gear out so nobody wastes the hour.
