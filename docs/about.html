The story

Why RTB exists

I studied exercise science because I wanted to know what actually works. Then I spent years on gym floors watching what actually happens. People turning up four times a week, doing cardio and a few machines, and looking exactly the same a year later. Not because they were lazy. Because nobody had given them a plan, a coach, or a number to beat.

Big gyms sell you access to equipment and leave you to work it out. One on one personal training gives you the coaching but costs more than most people can keep paying. There was a gap in the middle. A small room, a coach on every set, a real program, at a price a normal person can afford for years.

That's RTB. Capped at 12 so the coach can see every rep. No cardio gear, because when you've got three hours a week, every one of them goes on strength. First in Windsor, now Chelsea Heights too.

What we believe

Four things we won't budge on

01

Strength first

Muscle changes your shape, lifts your metabolism and keeps you independent at 70. Everything else is second.

02

Coaching, not supervision

A coach who watches you lift and fixes it, not a trainer who counts reps from across the room.

03

Numbers, not feelings

A working max on every lift, a weight on every set, a test every block. You know if it's working because you can see it.

04

Twelve, not forty

The cap is the product. Past twelve, coaching turns into crowd control and we won't do it.
