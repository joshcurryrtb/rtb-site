Questions

Before you book

Is the call a sales call?

No. It's fifteen minutes about you. Where you're at, what you want, and whether the Reset fits. If it does, Josh tells you how to start. If it doesn't, he tells you that too.

I've never lifted weights. Is that a problem?

It's the most common starting point we see. The Reset eases you in with a coach on every set and a program written for your level. You'll be fine.

What does unlimited actually mean?

Come to as many classes as you like across the 21 days. Most people settle at three or four a week. Every one is coached.

Which gym should I pick?

Whichever is closer to home or work. Same program, same coaching, same cap of 12 at both.

What happens after 21 days?

You rescan, retest and decide. Most people join. If you don't, you keep your program, your numbers and a month of the online program. No hard sell.

Can I try a class first instead?

Yes. Book one free class here. Some people would rather feel it than talk about it.
