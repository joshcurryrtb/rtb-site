The new program is live in TrainHeroic. It started today and runs six weeks, straight into the biggest test week of the year.

The idea is simple. Every Monday the bar gets heavier and the reps come down. Small steps, every week, so by December your body is ready for a number it has never hit before.

Here is how the main lifts build.

Those percentages come off your October test numbers. Week 1 should feel comfortable. That is the point. The weight does the work for you as the weeks go on, so do not chase it early.

Warm up sets are the same every week. In weeks 5 and 6 you get an extra heavy single at 85% before your working sets, so the top weights feel normal by the time you test.

Around the main lifts, the accessories have one job.

Core
A stronger trunk for deadlift and squat
Lots of core work this block. Hyperextensions, superman planks, knee to elbow planks, toe taps, Russian twists. A strong lower back and strong upper glutes are what hold your position when the bar gets heavy on Tuesday and Thursday.
 
Glutes
Drive out of the hole
Hip thrusts, Bulgarian split squats and banded crab walks. The upper glutes lock your hips in on deadlift and give you the drive at the bottom of a squat.
 
Triceps
Finish your bench press
Overhead tricep extensions, close grip work, push ups and floor press. Most missed bench presses die at the top. Stronger triceps get the bar there.

Rest times are in the app on every main lift, 2 min 15 on bench days and 2 min 30 on deadlift and squat days. Stick to them. Rushing sets in weeks 5 and 6 is how a 90% rep gets missed.

Three things to do this week.

One
Check your maxes in TrainHeroic
The percentages only work if your maxes are right. If a lift feels way too light or way too heavy in week 1, tell your coach and we fix the number.
 
Two

Three
