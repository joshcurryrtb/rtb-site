How it runs

Exactly what happens

10 before

Arrive ten minutes early. The coach meets you at the door, asks about injuries and what you're after, and gets you a spot at a rack.

First 10

Warm up with the group. Empty bar. The coach shows you the main lift for the day and where you'll start.

30 mins

The session. Main lift, then the supporting work. Every set watched. The coach sets your weights so you're never guessing.

Last 10

Finisher, a look around, and a straight answer on how to keep going if you want to.

Questions

Before you book

Is it really free?

Yes. No card, nothing to pay on the day. One class per person. We'd rather you see the floor than take our word for it.

Will I be thrown in with people who've trained for years?

You'll be in the same room, not on the same weights. The coach sets your loads and stays on you. Members were all new once and they know it.

What do I bring?

Runners, a drink bottle, clothes you can squat in. We supply the rest.

Can I book a second free class?

No. One is enough to know. After that it's the 21 Day Reset, which is unlimited sessions for three weeks.

I've got a bad back or a dodgy knee.

Tell the coach at the door and they'll work around it. If you're currently under treatment, check with your physio first.
